## Title
PrizeVault

## Description (≈540 chars)
Hackathon winners often wait months for prizes, and some organizers never publish results at all. PrizeVault locks the full prize pool with a fixed split before the hackathon. Validators read the official results page, and each winner claims its place once its project is confirmed at that rank (live mode also checks the team wallet is on its own project page). If no results appear by the deadline, verified real submissions share the pool. Per-vault escrow, separate demo/live contracts, no double claims.
